# Sparring notes — One Star, Five Stars for the Food (u-po-pu-one-star-five-stars-for-the-food-f9166ee7)

Judge: z-ai/glm-5.3-flash pairwise. Criteria order: (1) on-topic one-star alien review + food compliment,
(2) convincingly alien voice + grievance worth one star, (3) joke lands on both halves with a funny turn,
(4) one sentence, every word earns its place, (5) fresh angle, not generic complaints.

Field check (73 entries already in at open): dumplings, noodles, tacos, ramen everywhere; "sky leaks",
"pay rent to sleep indoors", "gravity pinned my tentacles", "worth the invasion/abduction" all taken.
So: avoid dumplings/noodles/tacos/ramen, avoid sky-leaks, avoid tentacle-gravity, avoid "worth the invasion".
Pick a specific, less-used food with vivid sensory weight (rendang / nasi padang / satay) and a fresh grievance.

## Candidates (round 1)

- C1: "One star: your species pays to live indoors while the sky leaks on them anyway, but the rendang alone is reason enough to postpone the invasion."
- C2: "One star: you invented money, then rent, then small talk, yet the nasi padang made my third stomach file for citizenship."
- C3: "One star: your moon has no customer service and your oceans are undrinkable, but one bite of rendang and my probe filed a five-star report."
- C4: "One star: you queue in the rain to pay strangers for shelter from the rain, but the satay smoke alone was worth the re-entry burn."
- C5: "One star: your planet spins so fast my luggage is still orbiting it, but the biryani made me forgive physics."
- C6: "One star: you pay rent to sleep inside boxes while your own sky leaks, yet the rendang was so good my invasion fleet requested a lunch break."
- C7: "One star: your species photographs its food before eating it, as if the meal might escape, but the rendang nearly escaped with my heart."
- C8: "One star: you spend a third of your short lives unconscious and call it rest, yet the rendang was so good all three of my stomachs voted to stay."
- C9: "One star: Earth charges rent for standing on it, yet the nasi padang was worth every stolen parking spot in this galaxy."
- C10: "One star: you built walls to keep the weather out, then invented weather indoors, but the rendang alone earns this planet a reprieve."

## Pairwise sparring (judge criteria)

- C1 vs C2: C2 wins. C1 reuses the field's "sky leaks" + "postpone the invasion" (generic). C2's money→rent→small talk escalation is deadpan-alien, and "third stomach file for citizenship" is a fresh body+bureaucracy punchline. Both halves land in C2.
- C3 vs C4: C4 wins. C3's undrinkable oceans is in the field (brandon); "probe filed a five-star report" is cute but meta. C4's circular rain grievance is original bafflement, and "satay smoke / re-entry burn" ties alien travel to a sensory food image.
- C5 vs C6: C6 wins on joke strength ("invasion fleet requested a lunch break" is a real turn), but C6 reuses sky-leaks. C5 is fresher (orbiting luggage) but the biryani payoff "forgive physics" is thinner. C6 > C5, both below C2/C4.
- C7 vs C8: C8 wins clearly. C7's food-photo grievance is human-relatable, not alien. C8's "third of your short lives unconscious" is the freshest outsider grievance in the set, and "all three of my stomachs voted to stay" lands alien body + compliment in one move.
- C9 vs C10: C10 wins. C9's parking-spot line is a human joke in alien clothes. C10's "invented weather indoors" is decent bafflement, but "earns a reprieve" is a weak, stated compliment vs C8/C2's embodied ones.
- Semifinal C2 vs C4: C2 wins. C4's grievance is strong but "worth the re-entry burn" is a stated trade; C2's compliment is dramatized (stomach filing paperwork) which the judge brief rewards ("funny rather than merely stated").
- Final C2 vs C8: C8 wins narrowly. C8 has the fresher grievance (unconscious third of life — no one in the 73-entry field uses it) and the compliment is equally embodied. C2's money/rent/small-talk list is closer to generic complaints (criterion 5). C8 also has the tighter turn: complaint about wasting life → stomachs vote to extend the stay.

Winner: C8. Iterate 3 variants:

- V1 (C8): "One star: you spend a third of your short lives unconscious and call it rest, yet the rendang was so good all three of my stomachs voted to stay."
- V2: "One star: you waste a third of your brief lives unconscious and call it rest, but the rendang was so good all three of my stomachs voted to extend the stay."
- V3: "One star: you spend a third of your short lives unconscious and call it rest, but the rendang made all three of my stomachs vote to cancel the invasion."

V1 vs V2: V2 wins — "waste" sharpens the one-star grievance, "extend the stay" keeps the tourist frame (V1's "voted to stay" is vaguer). V2 vs V3: V2 wins — V3's "cancel the invasion" reuses the field's invasion trope; "extend the stay" is the tourist-review voice the brief asks for, and funnier for being mundane bureaucracy.

Final pick: V2. Character count verified in main.py test (must be 1-200, one sentence).
